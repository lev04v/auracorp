CLINICAL BIOMECHANICS

The Science of Buoyancy, Hydrodynamics & Thermal Recovery.

Water is an extraordinary rehabilitation medium. When engineered with precision temperature control, laminarly directed resistance currents, and adjustable depth platforms, hydrotherapy accelerates neuromuscular healing without bearing painful joint stress.

Unweighted Buoyancy Gait Training

Water immersion up to the chest offloads up to 80–90% of dynamic skeletal body weight, enabling early post-surgical walking and gait re-education.

Hydrostatic Pressure & Edema Drainage

Uniform fluid compression enhances venous return, reduces chronic inflammation and limb swelling, and accelerates lymphatic metabolic clearance.

Contrast Temperature Vascular Pumping

Alternating between regulated cold plunges (4°C–10°C) and hot thermal hydrotherapy (38°C) stimulates rapid vasoconstriction and vasodilation for muscle flush.

Smooth Hydrodynamic Kinetic Resistance

Resistance scales naturally with patient velocity: double the movement speed generates quadruple the resistance with zero harsh deceleration shock.

Turnkey 3D Engineering

Custom hydraulic layouts, structural slab weight planning, and automated chemical dosing.
